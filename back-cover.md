For the back cover, I’d keep the historical hook—the lost pages and rediscovery—but quickly turn toward what makes Mary compelling for a contemporary contemplative reader.

What remains of the *Gospel of Mary* comes to us in fragments.

Its opening pages are lost. Four more disappear just as Mary begins recounting her vision of the risen Christ. Yet across the centuries, what survives still carries a striking message of inner freedom: desire does not own us, fear does not define us, and the voices that accuse us need not have the final word.

In this 28-day modern devotional, the surviving words of this ancient gospel become an invitation to slow down and listen. Each reading begins with a short passage from the text, followed by a contemplative reflection and guided prayer exploring themes of attachment, courage, spiritual perception, freedom, and the discovery of the divine within.

Along the way, Mary emerges as a courageous spiritual voice—comforting frightened disciples, turning their hearts toward the Good, and describing a soul that passes beyond Desire, Ignorance, and Wrath until at last it finds rest in silence.

We cannot recover the pages that were lost.

But perhaps we do not need a complete manuscript to hear what remains.

Come sit with the fragments.

Listen.

And discover what within you is already becoming free.
