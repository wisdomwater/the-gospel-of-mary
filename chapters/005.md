## Day 5 — Be at Peace

> *“That is why I said to you, ‘Be of good courage,’ and if you are discouraged, be encouraged in the presence of the different forms of nature.”*
>
> *“Let the one who has ears to hear, hear.”*

There are moments when peace seems dependent upon circumstances. We tell ourselves we will settle once the uncertainty is resolved, once the difficult conversation is over, once the future becomes clearer, or once the thing troubling us finally changes. Until then, we remain watchful, as though peace must wait for life to become safe enough to permit it.

The Savior offers a different possibility. “Be of good courage” is spoken in the presence of change, not after change has been overcome. The different forms of nature continue to arise and pass away around us. Bodies age, relationships change, plans unravel, seasons turn, and circumstances refuse to remain where we placed them. Courage does not require any of this to stop.

Perhaps courage, then, is less about becoming fearless and more about discovering that we can remain present while fear moves through us. We do not have to chase every anxious thought into the future or solve every uncertainty before allowing ourselves to rest. We can feel the ground beneath our feet, notice the breath entering and leaving the body, and return to the life that is actually here. The world may still be changing, but we do not have to change with every movement of the world.

This is a quieter courage than we often imagine. It does not announce itself loudly or conquer anything. Sometimes courage is simply refusing to abandon the present moment because another moment seems threatening. It is trusting that whatever tomorrow contains, we will meet it when it arrives rather than living it repeatedly in our imagination today.

“Let the one who has ears to hear, hear.” Perhaps hearing requires becoming quiet enough to notice what fear usually drowns out. Beneath the mind's predictions and rehearsals, there may already be a deeper stillness that does not depend upon knowing what comes next. We do not have to manufacture that peace. We may only need to stop running long enough to discover that it has been waiting for us here.

**Prayer**

> God of peace, when my mind runs ahead into what might happen, gently return me to what is here. Give me courage that does not depend upon certainty and peace that does not depend upon circumstances. Help me meet change without abandoning myself to fear, trusting that I need only receive this moment as it comes. Quiet me enough to hear the stillness beneath it all. Amen.
