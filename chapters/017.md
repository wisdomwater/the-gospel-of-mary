# Day 17 — I Was Bound, Though I Did Not Bind

> *“The soul said, ‘Why do you judge me, though I have not judged?’”*
>
> *“‘I was bound, though I did not bind. I was not recognized, but I have recognized that the All is being dissolved, both the earthly things and the heavenly.’”*

The soul does not deny that it was bound. There is no attempt to rewrite the past or pretend that captivity was freedom. “I was bound,” it says plainly. Yet it makes an equally important distinction: “I did not bind.” What happened to the soul, what surrounded it, and what constrained it do not tell the whole story of what the soul is.

There is wisdom in being able to hold both truths at once. We can acknowledge that we have been shaped by fear without deciding that we are fear. We can recognize patterns of craving, anger, avoidance, or self-protection without reducing ourselves to those patterns. We can even admit the harm we have caused while refusing the idea that our worst moment contains the final definition of us. Honest self-knowledge neither excuses nor condemns. It simply sees more completely.

Then the soul says something even more expansive: “I was not recognized, but I have recognized.” Ignorance could not see the soul clearly, but the soul has begun to see reality differently. It recognizes that everything it once treated as permanent is passing away. Earthly things and heavenly things alike are being dissolved. The structures that seemed absolute are temporary forms.

Perhaps this is why judgment begins to lose its power. So much judgment depends upon freezing something in place. We take a moment, a behavior, a belief, a success, or a failure and say, “This is what you are.” But if everything is moving, changing, and returning to its root, then no single moment can contain the whole truth of a person.

This does not make our choices meaningless. It makes transformation possible. If nothing about us could change, repentance would be pointless. If every failure revealed an unchangeable identity, there could be no liberation. But Mary’s soul lives in a world where forms dissolve and what has been bound can become free.

Perhaps we can offer ourselves and others the same spaciousness. We can tell the truth about what is without pretending that what is must remain forever. We can say, “I was bound,” and still keep walking.

**Prayer**

> God who sees me completely, teach me to tell the truth without turning truth into condemnation. Help me acknowledge what has bound me, what I have done, and what still needs healing without mistaking any of these things for my deepest identity. Give me the same spaciousness toward others. Where I have been bound, lead me toward freedom; where I have judged, teach me to see. Amen.
