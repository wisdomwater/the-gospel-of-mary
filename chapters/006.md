# Day 6 — Do Not Look Here or There

> *“Take care that no one leads you astray, saying, ‘Look here!’ or ‘Look there!’ For the Son of Man is within you.”*
>
> *“Follow him. Those who seek him will find him.”*

There is something deeply human about looking elsewhere. We imagine that what we most need must be located just beyond where we are now: in another experience, another teacher, another achievement, another stage of spiritual growth. Even our search for God can become another form of restlessness, a reaching toward some distant place where we imagine the sacred must surely be more present than it is here.

The Gospel of Mary interrupts that search with a startling claim: “The Son of Man is within you.” Whatever else these words may mean, they turn our attention inward. The way is not found by anxiously chasing every voice that announces, “Here it is!” We are invited instead to become quiet enough to recognize a presence that is already near, closer to us than the things we have been searching for.

This does not mean that teachers, traditions, communities, or sacred places have no value. They can help us recognize what we have forgotten. But perhaps their deepest purpose is not to make us dependent upon them, but to point beyond themselves. A good teacher eventually directs our attention toward the place where truth must finally be encountered: within the depths of our own awakened life.

There is freedom in this. We do not have to manufacture a spiritual experience or travel somewhere extraordinary before the journey can begin. We can become attentive here, in this body, in this room, in the ordinary circumstances of this day. The invitation is not merely to believe that the divine is near, but to become present enough to notice.

And then comes the simple instruction: “Follow him.” The inward journey is not withdrawal from life. If we recognize compassion within us, we follow it into the way we treat another person. If we recognize truth, we allow it to reshape the way we live. If we glimpse a deeper freedom beneath our fear and craving, we practice living from that freedom. The presence within becomes the path beneath our feet.

**Prayer**

> Christ within me, quiet my restless search for you somewhere else. Help me recognize your presence in the depths of this ordinary moment. Teach me to listen without grasping, to seek without striving, and to trust the light you have already placed within me. As I recognize that light, give me courage to follow where it leads. Amen.
