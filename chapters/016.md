# Day 16 — What Binds You?

> *“Then it came to the third power, which is called Ignorance.”*
>
> *It questioned the soul, saying, “Where are you going? You are bound by wickedness. Indeed, you are bound. Do not judge!”*

Ignorance speaks differently from Desire. Desire said, “You belong to me.” Ignorance says, “You are bound.” It looks at the soul and sees only captivity, as though whatever has entangled the soul must reveal the truth about what the soul is.

There is a kind of ignorance that comes from simply not knowing something, and there is no shame in that. But the Ignorance Mary describes seems deeper. It is a failure of perception. It looks at what is temporary and mistakes it for what is ultimate. It sees a person caught in a pattern and concludes that the pattern defines the person. It sees failure and calls the person a failure. It sees the chains but cannot imagine the possibility of freedom.

We can turn this gaze upon ourselves. When we repeat something we had hoped to leave behind, an old voice may quickly announce that nothing has really changed. When fear returns after a season of courage, we may decide that our courage was never genuine. When we discover another layer of woundedness, attachment, or selfishness within ourselves, we may wonder whether all our growth has been an illusion. Ignorance takes a moment in our story and mistakes it for the ending.

But being bound is not the same as belonging to the bonds. This is the insight the soul has already begun to discover. Desire could surround it without possessing it. The garment could cover it without becoming its identity. Now Ignorance sees the remaining entanglement and assumes that captivity is the final truth.

Contemplative awareness teaches us to look more patiently. We can acknowledge what binds us without denying the deeper movement toward freedom. We can tell the truth about our failures without turning them into definitions of ourselves. Transformation is rarely a clean ascent in which every old pattern disappears behind us forever. Often we meet the same places again, but with greater awareness, greater compassion, and a little more freedom to choose.

Ignorance says, “You are bound,” as though nothing more needs to be said. Wisdom notices the bonds and asks a different question: *What within you is already becoming free?*

**Prayer**

> God of wisdom, help me see myself more clearly than my failures allow. When I encounter the places where I am still bound, keep me from mistaking them for the whole truth about who I am. Give me honesty without condemnation, patience without resignation, and eyes to recognize the freedom already awakening within me. Lead me from ignorance into deeper seeing. Amen.
